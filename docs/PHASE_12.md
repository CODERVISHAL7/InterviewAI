# Phase 12 — HR Practice & Learning Recommendations

## Scope
- HR/behavioral question generation for HR interviews.
- Mixed interviews balance technical and HR/behavioral prompts.
- Learning recommendations derived from stored performance-report scores.
- Focus areas, recommended topics, action plan, and HR practice tips are persisted per user/interview.
- RLS restricts recommendation records to their owner.

## Privacy
No resume text or raw camera data is copied into the recommendation record. Recommendations use the stored interview performance report.
